about について

NSS Radio is a small independent music stream for daytime or nighttime pleasure — records, sets, and late-night selections broadcast from a rock named the Isle of Man, in the middle of the Irish Sea.

The station runs 24/7, with live sessions when a DJ is in and curated selections filling the gaps so something is always on air.

Rooted in New Social Studios //

You’ll find us on YouTube as @corkwinglive, where we’re not monetised, so, if you’d like to support you can buy me a coffee to keep the wheels running.
